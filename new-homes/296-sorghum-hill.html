About this home

Modern farmhouse charm, room to breathe and wide-open Texas skies.

A new four-bedroom modern farmhouse on approximately 1.876 acres at The Elms. The main level has an open kitchen, dining and living area with a dark olive island, a primary suite and a second bedroom. Upstairs is a family room, two more bedrooms and a full bath. A 468 sq ft covered front porch and a 278 sq ft covered back patio make the most of the country views.
